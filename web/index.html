1 · Your box

Your box is saved in this browser under your profile ID. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.

How to record your box
  1. Filter the box to the units you want considered, and keep one sort order.
  2. Start a screen recording, open the first unit's detail page, then flick to the next unit, pausing about a second on each.
  3. Go through every unit once, without going back, then stop the recording and upload it here.

Tested on 1180×2556 iPhone recordings. Other screen sizes may not read correctly yet; anything doubtful is shown for you to confirm.
